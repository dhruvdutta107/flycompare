About FlyCompare

FlyCompare is a student project that combines Skyscanner's clean, low-clutter fare comparison with the trusted booking flow of IRCTC Air. It explores how one consistent interface can reduce the cognitive load travellers feel when comparing fares, booking and managing a trip.

Terms of use

This is a non-commercial coursework project. Flights, fares, airlines and offers are simulated sample data; no real payments are processed and no real flights are booked or ticketed. Do not enter real card details.

Privacy

We store the details you enter — passenger names, dates of birth, contact e-mail and phone, and bookings — in our database so you can retrieve them with your PNR. Account passwords are salted and hashed. Card numbers, CVVs and UPI ids are validated and then discarded; only the card type and last four digits are kept. We use a single, secure session cookie to keep you logged in and local storage for your theme, recent searches and saved promo code. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
